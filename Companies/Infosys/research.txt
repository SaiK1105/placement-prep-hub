CONFIDENCE B — WEB RESEARCH, MULTIPLE INDEPENDENT SOURCES AGREE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Structure: ~7 sections, ~60 questions total.

  Mathematical / Quantitative Ability   ~10 Q
  Analytical / Logical Reasoning        ~15 Q
  Verbal Ability                        ~20 Q
  Pseudocode                            ~5 Q
  Puzzle Solving                        ~4 Q
  English Grammar                       ~5 Q
  English Writing (essay)               1 Q

AGREED ACROSS SOURCES (treat as reliable):
  * NO NEGATIVE MARKING on MCQ sections
  * SECTIONAL CUT-OFFS APPLY — every section is independently qualifying.
    >>> THIS IS THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT STRATEGIC FACT. <<<
    Unlike TCS, a strong section cannot rescue a weak one. Failing any one
    section can end the drive regardless of total score.
  * Adaptive test pattern reported for 2026 (questions harden as you answer
    correctly). Implication: early accuracy matters more than late volume.
  * PSEUDOCODE IS MCQ, NOT WRITING CODE. You read a C-like block, trace it,
    and predict the output. No compiler, no typing code.

DURATION: 120 MINUTES, 60 QUESTIONS. Per-section table below reconciles
arithmetically (25+35+20+10+10+10+10 = 120), which the earlier 95/110/130
figures did not. Sections are INDIVIDUALLY TIMED — no banking time.

  Technical / Mathematical Ability   10 Q   35 min   (3.5 min/Q - luxurious)
  Logical Ability                    15 Q   25 min   (1.7 min/Q - HARDEST section)
  Verbal                             20 Q   20 min   (60 sec/Q - BRUTAL, only rushed section)
  Pseudo Code                         5 Q   10 min   (2 min/Q)
  Puzzle Solving                      4 Q   10 min   (2.5 min/Q)
  English Grammar                     5 Q   10 min   (2 min/Q)
  English Writing (essay)             1 Q   10 min

PACING INSIGHT: Verbal at 60 s/Q is the only genuinely rushed section.
Maths at 3.5 min/Q is enormous. Rushing Maths is self-harm; the real risk is
carrying Verbal panic into the next section.

** CORRECTION: THE TEST IS NON-ADAPTIVE. ** An earlier source said adaptive;
PrepInsta's syllabus page states non-adaptive explicitly, and fixed
per-section timings support it. Consequence: a hard question does NOT signal
you are scoring well, and there is no opening-accuracy calibration effect.
Skip and return freely within a section.

CODING: THE REGULAR OLT CONTAINS NO CODING. Confirmed — the 7-section paper
above is the whole sitting. The SP/DSE coding test is a SEPARATE ~3-hour exam
held afterwards for premium-tier consideration.

ONE SOURCE STATES the OLT pattern is identical for on-campus, off-campus and
referral drives. Raises confidence this applies to the campus sitting.

Also reported: "Logical Reasoning is the most difficult section."

SP / DSE CODING ROUND (SEPARATE SITTING, AFTER THE OLT)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
First-hand account, off-campus Jan 2026, offline test centre, 500+ candidates,
4 problems:
  Easy    - query-based operations on a 2D list; LENGTHY statement, careful
            parsing required
  Medium  - TWO-POINTER + SLIDING WINDOW
  Hard    - GRAPH combined with STATE-BASED DP
  Complex - TREE with range queries, difficult edge cases
Guidance: solve at least 3 of 4 to stay competitive.

TIERING (explicit, from the same account):
  Easy + Medium  -> DSE      ~7 LPA
  + Hard         -> SP L3    ~11 LPA
  + Complex      -> SP L2    ~16 LPA  /  SP L3 higher band ~21 LPA

Candidate quote: "passing sample test cases does not guarantee final
acceptance" — hidden tests and edge cases decide it. THIS IS EXACTLY THE
TCS FAILURE MODE FROM 17 AUG. Same discipline required.

two slots, medium-to-hard overall.
