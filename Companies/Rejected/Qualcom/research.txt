=== THE HEADLINE: NO CODING ROUND. IT'S ALL MCQ. [MULTI] ===
The Indian campus OA is consistently reported as MCQ-ONLY — no coding editor.
Programming is tested via OUTPUT PREDICTION and DEBUG-SPOTTING MCQs.
This is a fundamentally different exam from Tredence/Honeywell/Staples.

=== FORMAT [MULTI, ~6 sources] ===
60 questions / 90 minutes total
3 sections x 20 MCQ each
30 MINUTES PER SECTION, SECTIONALLY LOCKED (cannot return to a finished section)
Sectional cutoffs applied IN ADDITION to overall score [SINGLE, BITS Pilani]

  SECTION 1 — Quantitative Aptitude + Logical Reasoning
    speed/distance, ratio & proportion, percentages, permutations, mixtures,
    number systems, Venn diagrams, data interpretation. Reported EASY-MEDIUM.

  SECTION 2 — Programming MCQs  *** REPORTED HARDEST ***
    C/C++ OUTPUT PREDICTION, error/debug spotting, POINTERS, STORAGE CLASSES,
    OOPS concepts, number-base conversion.
    *** LANGUAGE IS C/C++ (some Java/OOPS). PYTHON DOES NOT HELP HERE. ***
    *** Python is NOT reported as an OA language anywhere. ***

  SECTION 3 — Domain/Core, CHOICE-BASED
    *** IF A CHOICE APPEARS, TAKE COMPUTER SCIENCE ***
    CS-option contents: OS, file systems, RDBMS (Oracle/MySQL), networking
    basics, routing, stacks/queues, linked lists, trees, sorting, DBMS.
    Maps well onto the OneIT JD stack.

    CONTRADICTION on what the options actually are (varies by year/drive):
      - "Computer Science or Electronics" (PrepInsta)
      - "Communication / Data Interpretation / Digital" (GfG on-campus intern)
      - "CS Core Subjects" only (GfG, different post)
      - "Data Interpretation & Verbal" (GfG intern, Jul-2025)
      - "Electronics / CS / Mechanical" (hitbullseye)

NO SQL section, NO subjective/essay, NO separate verbal-English section
reported for the Indian campus test. [MULTI, absence-based]

=== PREP IMPLICATIONS (differs sharply from every prior OA) ===
1. NO Python DSA drilling helps Section 2. Revise C/C++ output prediction:
   pointers, arrays-vs-pointers, storage classes (static/extern/auto/register),
   pre/post increment, operator precedence, OOPS (virtual, inheritance,
   constructors/destructors order).
2. Aptitude is a full third of the paper and sectionally locked -> timed
   practice matters more here than in any previous OA.
3. CS core theory (OS/DBMS/CN) is a third of the paper. Not coding — theory.
4. Negative marking changes strategy: eliminate then answer; skip true blanks.
5. Sectional lock means you CANNOT bank time from an easy section for a hard
   one. Pace inside each 30-min block.
