--- SQL QUESTION (actual) ---
  Tables: customer(id, firstname, lastname, email, phoneno, amount)
          orderList(id, date, phoneno, Amount)
  Ask: show all columns of customer AND orderList
       where date > '2023-09-01'
       order by name
  Shape: INNER JOIN on phoneno + WHERE on date + ORDER BY name.
  Note: join key is phoneno (not id) — the two id columns are unrelated.

--- CODING PROBLEM 1 (actual) — GRAPH / PATH WITH BUDGET ---
  Input:
    5 4                 (5 cities, 4 routes)
    1 2 10
    2 3 10
    3 4 20
    4 5 10
    amount = 120
  Output: 1 2 3 4 3 2 1
  Shape: weighted undirected graph, travel out from source and return,
  total cost must stay within budget; print the route taken.
  NOTE: exact objective still ambiguous from the recall — going 1..5 and
  back costs 100 (<=120) yet the printed answer stops at city 4 (cost 80).
  Need the full statement to pin the objective. Ask candidate.

--- CODING PROBLEM 2 (actual) — MATRIX MIN PATH SUM ---
  Input:
    3 3
    1 4 3
    2 5 4
    7 5 9
  Output: 21   (path 1 -> 2 -> 5 -> 4 -> 9)
  Shape: classic LeetCode 64 "Minimum Path Sum" — DP grid, move right/down
  only, dp[i][j] = grid[i][j] + min(dp[i-1][j], dp[i][j-1]).
  Verified: min over all right/down paths of this grid IS 21.
