CONFIDENCE B — FORMAT (multiple independent sources agree on the shape)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Amazon SDE Intern OA is a multi-part single sitting:

  1. CODING CHALLENGE
     - 2 problems
     - 70-90 minutes total (sources report both; 70 min is the more common
       figure for intern, 90 min for full-time SDE)
     - Difficulty: medium. Intern set reported slightly easier than SDE-1.
     - Platform runs your code against visible + hidden tests.

  2. WORK SIMULATION  (~20-30 min)
     - "Day in the life at Amazon" — you receive emails/messages and choose
       responses. Scenario-based, no coding.
     - Reported to weight OWNERSHIP and BIAS FOR ACTION most heavily.

  3. WORK STYLE SURVEY / ASSESSMENT  (~10-15 min)
     - Agree/disagree statements mapped to the 16 Leadership Principles.
     - Not scored as right/wrong but IS used; consistency matters.

Both are Amazon-flavoured story wrappers over standard patterns —
consistent with the note that Amazon dresses hashing/counting problems in
warehouse/inventory/delivery narratives.
