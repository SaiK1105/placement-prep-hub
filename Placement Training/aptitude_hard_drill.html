📌 The setups worth memorising

  • Two-scenario relative motion (Q2): write both scenarios as equations, subtract them — the unknown starting distance cancels.
  • Proportional-reduction (Q1): "reduction ∝ √n" means speed = base − k√n. Find k from the given case, then solve for the limit.
  • Repeated replacement (Q6): final = initial × (1 − r/V)ⁿ. Never simulate it step by step under a clock.
  • Pairwise work rates (Q8): summing all three pairs gives 2× the combined rate — halve it before inverting.
  • Overlapping averages (Q14): first-half + second-half counts the middle element twice; subtract the true total.
  • Clock right angles (Q18): minute hand gains 5.5°/min; solve |30H − 5.5t| = 90 for both roots — there are almost always two.
